## Completed P1 work

### AI two-step evaluation reliability

- Added fail-closed First Step evaluation JSON policy in `SurveyAiPolicy`.
- Required schema:
  - `score`: unquoted integer in `1..100`
  - `missing_points`: array of non-blank strings
  - `followup_needed`: JSON boolean
- High score (`90..100`) requires empty `missing_points` and `followup_needed=false`.
- Low score (`1..89`) requires non-empty `missing_points` and `followup_needed=true`.
- Malformed, missing, wrongly typed, or contradictory values resolve to `FAILURE` and do not generate or persist a follow-up or consume capacity.
- First Step validation and Second Step follow-up generation are separated for configured TWO_STEP nodes.
- Stale/cancelled AI chains cannot overwrite replacement-chain terminal state.

### Whisper recording reliability

- Added explicit recording-session ownership in `WhisperSpeechController`.
- Recorder, WAV output, survey/question context, worker job, callbacks, cleanup, export, and transcription results are session-owned.
- Stale callbacks cannot mutate a replacement recording session.
- `stopRecording()` timeout marks the session poisoned; a new recording is blocked until the recorder close operation actually completes.
- Recovery after a completed close is covered by instrumentation tests.

### TTS integration

- Reviewed and merged remote commit `6da30c4`.
- Adds config-driven Android platform TTS for question read-aloud, autoplay, and replay controls.
- User manually verified TTS playback on the connected Pixel 9a.
