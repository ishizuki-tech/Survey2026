What's New

Upgrade LiteRT-LM runtime to 0.18.0 (#103)

## Summary
- Upgrade LiteRT-LM Android runtime dependency from `0.17.1` to `0.18.0`.
- Single production change in `gradle/libs.versions.toml` (+1/-1); existing Gemma 3n E4B GPU model remains unchanged.
- Closes no issue automatically; tracks #103 pending final risk acceptance.

## Verification
- `:app:compileDebugKotlin`, `:app:compileDebugAndroidTestKotlin`, `:app:testDebugUnitTest`, and `:app:assembleDebug` passed in the clean review worktree.
- Exact resolved dependency: `com.google.ai.edge.litertlm:litertlm-android:0.18.0`.
- `git diff --check` passed.
- The clean worktree build excluded `verifySwahiliAsrModel` because ignored Whisper model assets were absent; the earlier audit worktree completed an asset-bearing build and verified Whisper assets.
- Prior Galaxy S25 E4B validation demonstrated initialization, cold/warm inference, teardown/reinitialization, cancellation/recovery, and LiteRT → Whisper → LiteRT handoff.
- Swahili EVAL/FOLLOWUP aggregate outcomes matched the 0.17.1 reference (29/30 parse-valid, 25/30 decision matches, 17/20 follow-ups accepted).

## Known risks and merge constraints
- Gemma 3n E4B GPU inference caused severe system-wide memory pressure and broad background-process LMKD reclamation on Galaxy S25 with **both** 0.17.1 and 0.18.0.
- A single process-cold pair showed no material Graphics PSS or TTFT regression, but does **not** establish statistical equivalence. Swap PSS was higher on 0.18.0 (+11.7 MiB after initialization, +17.3 MiB after cold inference).
- The 0.18.0 benchmark was intentionally stopped after normal teardown and before reinitialization due to memory pressure; do not interpret that run as a complete lifecycle test.
- The device's `com.negi.survey` package was previously replaced accidentally by a debug-signed 0.18.0 build. Production package recovery, including approval of data-preserving operations and the `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED` upload/recovery side effects, remains a **separate unfinished task**. Do not treat this S25 as clean production validation until recovery is completed.
- No test-only audit instrumentation changes are included in this PR.

## Before merge
- [ ] Explicitly accept and document the existing E4B/LMKD device-level risk and remaining 0.18.0 performance uncertainty.
- [ ] Confirm that package recovery remains tracked and is required before clean production-device validation.
- [ ] Review PR diff and CI; do not merge automatically.
